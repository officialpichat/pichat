Frequently asked questions

How do I get the blue tick?

Pay ₹50 via UPI from the "Blue Tick Verification" section above and submit your request — an admin reviews it and grants it. You can also unlock it for free by referring 12 friends who join with your referral link, or by redeeming 1200 coins.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use "Forgot password" on the login screen to reset it via email. If you signed up with Google and never set a password, just continue logging in with the Google button, or email us to set one up.

I forgot my two-factor authentication PIN.

Email us at officialpichat@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we'll have an admin reset it, so you can log in and set a new one.

Someone is sending me inappropriate messages or content.

You can block them from their profile, or leave/report a group from its Group info screen. For urgent safety concerns, email us with the username and details and we'll take action.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

We'd love to hear it — email us a quick description (and a screenshot if you have one) at officialpichat@gmail.com.
