What is Pichat?

Pichat is a social media app where being active pays off. Post photos, upload reels with music and filters, send snaps from a built-in camera, post stories and chat one-to-one or in groups — all in one place. Along the way you collect coins for posting, following people and simply spending time in the app. Use them to unlock a blue tick for your profile or, once you qualify, withdraw them to your UPI. Pichat is designed to feel fast, clean and personal — no clutter, just your people and your content.

Everything you need, in one app

From your first post to your first thousand followers — Pichat has the tools.

Posts & Stories

Share photos with filters and a background remover, add a story, and see what the people you follow are up to in your feed.

Reels with music

Upload short videos, pick a song, add filters and volume control, and tag a collaborator. Friends can even remix your reel.

Snap camera

A full-screen camera with live filters, flash, a self-timer and music — snap it and send it straight to a friend.

Chat & calls

One-to-one and group chats, reactions, disappearing messages, shared reels and voice or video calls.

Trending & search

Find people fast, and explore the videos that are going viral right now — picked to match what you like to watch.

Coins & rewards

Earn coins for being active, redeem them for a blue tick, or withdraw to UPI once you qualify. Refer friends for a bonus.

Blue tick verification

Get your profile verified so people know it's really you — by redeeming coins or through a quick payment request.

Privacy you control

Private accounts, limited interactions, active-status and screenshot-alert controls, plus Light, Dark and Auto themes.

Questions, answered

Is Pichat free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and using Pichat is free. Optional extras, like requesting a blue tick by payment, are clearly shown inside the app.

How do I earn coins?

You earn coins by posting, uploading reels, following people and spending active time in the app. Coins stay valid for 30 days from the day you earn them, so the more consistently you use Pichat, the more you keep.

How can I get the blue tick?

You can redeem coins for it from Settings, or send a verification request with a payment reference for the admin to review. Inviting friends with your referral link also earns you coins towards it.

Can I withdraw my coins as money?

Yes, to UPI, once your account has the blue tick and at least 5,000 followers. The rate is 10 coins = ₹1, with a minimum withdrawal of 5,000 coins.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Pichat runs in your phone or computer browser — just tap Open App.

Who do I contact if I need help?

Message our helpline on WhatsApp at +91 73004 36058 and we'll get back to you.

Need help? We're a message away.

Questions about your account, coins, blue tick or anything else — reach out on WhatsApp and we'll help you out.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the blue tick?

Pay ₹459 via UPI from the "Blue Tick Verification" section above and submit your request — an admin reviews it and grants it. You can also unlock it for free by redeeming 1200 coins — which you can earn by using Pichat, or by referring 12 friends who join with your referral link (12 referrals = 1200 coins).

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use "Forgot password" on the login screen to reset it via email. If you signed up with Google and never set a password, just continue logging in with the Google button, or email us to set one up.

I forgot my two-factor authentication PIN.

Email us at officialpichat@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we'll have an admin reset it, so you can log in and set a new one.

Someone is sending me inappropriate messages or content.

You can block them from their profile, or leave/report a group from its Group info screen. For urgent safety concerns, email us with the username and details and we'll take action.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

We'd love to hear it — email us a quick description (and a screenshot if you have one) at officialpichat@gmail.com.
